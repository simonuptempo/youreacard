# Voice lines to record

Ten lines per character. Lines 0-2 are already recorded; record **3-9** next.
Name each file `<character>_<number>.mp3` (for example `bouncer_5.mp3`) and send them over;
they get trimmed, volume-matched and put in `audio/voice_real/`. A line plays only once its recording exists.

**Other-gender voices** (for players who put their own face on a card): same lines, named
`<character>_alt_<number>.mp3` (for example `locksmith_alt_0.mp3`). Players pick it in My Cards → + Your voice.
